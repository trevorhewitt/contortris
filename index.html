How to play

The goal

Weird pieces fall from the top. Fill a whole row with blocks and the row is destroyed. Don't let the pile reach the top.

Controls

Touch: tap the left or right side to move, tap the top to rotate, tap the bottom middle to drop faster. Hold to keep going.

Keyboard: ← → move, ↑ rotate, ↓ drop faster, Space pause.

Powerups (Easy and Normal)

Some pieces are powerups: their name glows rainbow and says what they do. While one falls, the cells it will affect light up where it is going to land.

Powerups come in four classes (Dissolverz, Gravitizerz, Expanderz and Phantomz), introduced one class at a time as you play, each with a quick intro; once a class is in, any of its pieces can turn up. Once two classes are in, Comboz (two powerups in one) start turning up too. When you're in trouble, powerups turn up more often and the more helpful ones get picked. The pause menu explains every class you've met so far.

Modes

Easy: powerups, and none of the giant pieces.
Normal: the full game, with powerups. Watch out for the giants.
Extreme: the original. No powerups, no mercy.

Achievements

Silly things unlock silly achievements. Some are secret. See them all from the main menu.
