How to play

Pieces fall. Fill a row to destroy it. Don't let the pile reach the top.

Controls

Touch: tap the left or right side to move, tap the top to rotate, tap the bottom middle to drop faster. Hold to keep going. Double tap the bottom to drop straight down.

Keyboard: ← → or A D move, ↑ or W rotate, ↓ or S drop faster, Shift use the next Extra Shiny Piece, Space or Esc pause. Double press ↓ or S to drop straight down.
