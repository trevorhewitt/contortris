How to play

Weird pieces fall. Fill a row to destroy it. Don't let the pile reach the top.

Controls

Touch: tap the left or right side to move, tap the top to rotate, tap the bottom middle to drop faster. Hold to keep going. Triple tap the bottom to drop straight down.

Keyboard: ← → move, ↑ rotate, ↓ drop faster, Space pause. Press ↓ three times quickly to drop straight down.
